# ADR-NNNN: Decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** Project Owner; named maintainers/reviewers
- **Scope:** Affected phases and modules
- **Decision IDs:** Linked rows from the decision register

## Context

What decision is needed, what constraints make it consequential, and which requirements own the behavior?

## Evidence

### Facts

- Verifiable observations with primary or inspected-source links.

### Inferences

- Design conclusions drawn from those facts, with assumptions named.

### Unknowns

- Unverified device, network, provider, cost, performance, or security questions.

## Decision

State the chosen rule precisely enough to test. Distinguish current implementation authority from future seams.

## Consequences

### Benefits

- What becomes simpler or safer.

### Costs and risks

- What remains trusted, unavailable, operationally expensive, or harder to change.

## Alternatives considered

- Alternative and why it was not selected now.

## Security and privacy effect

Assets, actors, trust-boundary change, and claims this decision does or does not support.

## Validation and revisit trigger

Named tests, evidence needed before release, and the event that requires new research or a superseding ADR.
