## Trademark and provenance boundary

Facts:

- “Our Poker Table” is the owner-approved product-facing name and identity.
- No trademark registration or current availability is asserted by this
  repository.

  geometry.

Unknowns:

- trademark similarity and availability in intended territories and classes;

Before an official commercial launch, obtain appropriate name and logo
clearance for the intended markets and uses. Preserve dated source and approval
records so later review can distinguish independent project design from
