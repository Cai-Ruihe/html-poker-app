# Support

There is no released application or production support service yet.

Once the public repository exists, use its issue templates for reproducible bugs and feature proposals. Include version/commit, browser, device, operating system, mode, network type, expected behavior, observed behavior, and minimized reproduction steps. Remove names, invitations, credentials, private cards, deck order, and raw developer logs.

Security and card-privacy reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and must not be posted publicly. Conduct reports follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

This is community open-source software provided without warranty under Apache-2.0. Maintainers may help where capacity permits, but no response-time or compatibility promise exists outside a published support policy for a release.
